# NineSync: the rules for building

The whole brand guide (the moods and color with contrast, the client color builder, type,
shape, motion with live demos, voice, do and don't, a sample screen, the tokens to download)
is the brand kit{__KIT_URL__ ? <>: <a href={__KIT_URL__} target="_top">open the brand kit</a></> : <>, in the package's <code>kit/</code> folder (<code>yarn workspace @rnine/ninesync dev-kit</code>)</>}.
These are the rules to keep in mind while you build.

## The client's color

- Every app wears its client's brand color: `ClientTheme colors={{ brand }}`, once, inside
  `ThemeProvider`. Check the colors first (the builder in the brand kit, or `syncColors()`).
- **Never change a client's color to make it pass.** When it fails in a mood (text under
  4.5:1), ask the client for a second color for that mood (`light` or `dark`). The check
  suggests one; it stands in until they give theirs.
- The brand color is for what you do: one filled main action per view, links, focus rings.
  The **pale tone** (`r9.$mark`) is decoration: the mark under titles and tabs, highlights,
  the wash after a save. Never text.
- Greys lean toward the client's hue; status colors (success, warning, danger) don't. Status
  is never color alone: a word goes with it.

## Color

- Use the mood roles (`r9.$accent`, `--r9-surface`...), never raw colors. Both moods are complete.
- NineSync's own roles (`r9.$mark`, `$mark-soft`, `$mark-line`, `$glow`, `$fg-strong`...) come
  through `@rnine/ninesync/scss`; a new one goes in its `@forward ... show` list and in `syncMood`.

## Shape

- 12px corners on buttons, fields and cards (`r9.$corner-md`, `$corner-lg`), 8px on chips and
  checkboxes (`$corner-sm`). Nothing is cut or folded. `r9.corner()` works too, but its
  clip-path hides the glow and the focus ring: on anything that glows or takes focus, use
  `border-radius: r9.$corner-md`.
- Hairline edges, no drop shadows. The Sync glow (`r9.glow-hover`) is the only shadow, on hover.

## Type

- DM Sans for everything, DM Mono for codes (SKUs, order numbers) and figures that must line up.
- Headlines are moderate: weight 650, −0.025em. Use the type roles (`@include r9.type(h2)`), not raw sizes.

## Motion

- Subtle but alive: titles draw their mark, the screen cascades in, counts count, the mark
  glides between tabs and pages, a save washes what changed. Only `LiveDot` moves on its own.
- The tuned values are the defaults: don't pass new ones without a reason.
- Content never waits on an effect; everything stops for reduced motion; effects never take clicks.

## Voice

Plain, short and specific, like a good colleague. Say what happened and what to do next
("Received 30 × Angle bar. 2 materials are still below their reorder point."), not how great it is.

## Accessibility

Every text role meets WCAG 2.2 AA on its surfaces in both moods, for the default colors (the
tests check) and for any client colors that pass the checks. Tabs and the side nav keep core's
keyboard support. Counts that animate read as their final number.
