# Ninefold: principles

Ninefold is for shops and sites with a little magic in them: a bookshop for fantasy
readers, a maker of candles or maps, a tea room, a tabletop game. It should feel like a
desk you'd want to sit at: paper, ink, wax, a candle, the window open on a garden. Cozy,
crafted, a little enchanted. Never twee, never a costume.

## Two moods: Glint and Gloam

- **Glint** is the day: honey-colored paper, iron-gall ink, claret wax, leaves drifting
  past the window.
- **Gloam** is the night: a walnut desk by candlelight, rose ink, amber light, fireflies.

Auto follows the clock: Glint by day (06:00-18:00) and, at night, Gloam when the
system is dark. Visitors can choose either one. Both moods are complete: nothing exists
in only one of them, it only changes its light.

## Ink and wax

- **Ink** (the accent) is for what you do: the main action, the selected stop, a focus
  ring, the pen line under a link. One inked thing per view is the main action.
- **Wax** (the signal) is for what's live: stock, a date that matters, the seal. Never a
  second brand color.
- Ninefold's own roles carry the rest: the lining of a folded corner, wet and dry ink,
  fireflies and motes, leaves, stars, the window's light.

## The look

- **Soft corners**, rounded to their size. Only cards are folded: the top-right corner is
  turned over, and it lifts toward the pointer.
- **Paper**: pressed fibers and a fine grain sit behind every page.
- **Fraunces** (soft and a little wonky) for headings, **Literata** for reading, **Courier
  Prime** for labels, **Pinyon Script** for a signature, never for running text.
- **A seal** for the mark: the app's own mark, pressed into wax, a sprig behind it.

## Motion

Everything moves the way something real would: leaves fall, fireflies blink and circle,
ink is wet then dries, a seal is pressed. Effects answer the person using the page or drift
slowly enough to ignore. Content never waits on an animation, and all of it stops for
reduced motion. See **Brand/Motion** for the tuned values.

## Voice

Warm, plain and specific, like a note tucked into a parcel. Say what the thing is
("Signed, sprayed edges, ships in January"), not how magical it is. A little wonder is
welcome in names and small touches ("The lantern is resting"); instructions stay plain.

## Accessibility

- Every text color meets WCAG 2.2 AA on the surfaces it sits on, in both moods (the
  conformance tests check every pair).
- Status is never color alone: a word or an icon goes with it.
- Effects are decorative and hidden from screen readers; titles that animate always read
  as their final text. The lantern trail is a tab list with arrow keys.
- Writing on the page (`Ink`) is opt-in, never starts on text, links or controls, and is
  off with reduced motion.
