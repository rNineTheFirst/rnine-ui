# Ninefold: the rules for building

The whole brand guide (the moods and color with contrast, type, shape and paper, the seal,
motion with live demos, voice, do and don't, a sample bookshop, the tokens and the seal to
download) is the brand kit{__KIT_URL__ ? <>: <a href={__KIT_URL__} target="_top">open the brand kit</a></> : <>, in the package's <code>kit/</code> folder (<code>yarn workspace @rnine/ninefold dev-kit</code>)</>}.
These are the rules to keep in mind while you build.

## Color

- Use the mood roles (`r9.$accent`, `--r9-surface`...), never raw colors. Both moods are complete.
- **Ink** (the accent) is for what you do: one inked main action per view, the selected
  stop, focus rings, the pen line under a link.
- **Wax** (the signal) is for what's live: stock, a date that matters, the seal. Never a
  second brand color.
- Ninefold's own roles (`r9.$wax`, `$lining`, `$ink-wet`, `$mote`, `$leaf`, `$star`, `$light`...)
  come through `@rnine/ninefold/scss`; a new one goes in its `@forward ... show` list.

## Shape and paper

- `@include r9.corner($size)`: soft corners (`sm` 6px, `md` 12px, `lg` 18px). Nothing is cut.
- **Only cards fold** (`r9.card-corner(lg)`, or `Card` / `TiltCard`): never buttons, chips or images.
- Hairline edges. The only shadows are under a fold and under a letter.
- The paper is `body::before`/`::after`: don't give both `html` and `body` a background,
  or a full-size wrapper one, or it covers the paper.

## Type

- Fraunces for headings, Literata for reading, Courier Prime for labels (uppercase, tracked),
  Pinyon Script for a signature or a flourish, **never running text**.
- Use the type roles (`@include r9.type(h2)`), not raw sizes.

## Motion

- The tuned values are the defaults: don't pass new ones without a reason.
- Effects answer the person or drift slowly; content never waits on them; all of it stops
  for reduced motion; effects never take clicks.
- `HeroLight` needs its hero positioned but not a stacking context. `Ink` is opt-in.

## Voice

Warm, plain and specific. Say what the thing is ("Signed, sprayed edges, ships in January"),
not how magical it is. Instructions stay plain.

## Accessibility

Every text role meets WCAG 2.2 AA on its surfaces in both moods (the tests check). Status
is never color alone. The lantern trail is a tab list with arrow keys.
