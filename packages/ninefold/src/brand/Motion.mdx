# Motion

Ninefold's effects fill the roles every library has, and add a few of its own:

| Role | By day (Glint) | At night (Gloam) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Backdrop` | Leaves drift down; the pointer is a breeze | Fireflies circle the pointer, scatter, flash |
| `TextReveal` | Letters grow up from the line | Fireflies gather into the words |
| `Mark` | The seal, pressed again on hover; the sprig sways | The same, with two fireflies circling it |
| `swapIn` / `swapOut` | Leaves carry the old page off and the new one in | Fireflies do |
| `PageEffects` | A click scatters leaves; dew catches the light | A click scatters fireflies |
| `HeroLight` | Sun through a window, drifting | A candle's glow, breathing, and a starry sky |
| `Ink` (opt-in) | Wet ink that dries and fades | Glowing by candlelight |

## The tuned values

These came from tuning on the sample bookshop, and they're the defaults.

| What | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Fireflies / leaves | 0.5x / 0.4x per area; leaves at 0.6x size |
| Fireflies | brightness 0.5x; circle the pointer 0.1x; scatter 0.2x; a flash wave every 5 s at rest |
| Title gather | 480 fireflies, 1100 ms |
| Title grow | 640 ms a letter, 10 ms apart |
| Folded corner | 26 → 60 px, from 450 px away, follows at 0.4 |
| Ink | 2 px pen, stays 1 s; touch: hold 300 ms |
| Lantern | walks at 1.2x; a mote behind it on 20% of frames |
| Swarm swap | 80 fireflies or leaves, at 0.8x the base timings |
| Wax stamp / seal | ring spreads to 2.4x; sprig sways 8° |
| Pen underline | 500 ms |
| Night sky | 0.8x stars |
| Click burst | 5 leaves or 6 fireflies |
| Field journal | a note every 6.5 s |
| Hero light | window 0.6x; candle glow 0.9x; flicker 0.5 |

## Rules

- Effects answer the person using the page, or drift slowly enough to ignore.
- Content never waits: a title is readable the moment it's there, a page is usable while
  its swarm settles.
- Everything stops for reduced motion (the system setting, the viewer's Still, or
  `ThemeProvider reducedMotion`): the backdrop becomes a still scene, the rest simply appears.
- Effects never take clicks and are hidden from screen readers.
