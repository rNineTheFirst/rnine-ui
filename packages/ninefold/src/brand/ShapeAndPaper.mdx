# Shape and paper

## Corners

- `@include r9.corner($size)`: soft corners, rounded to their size: `sm` 6px (chips,
  inputs), `md` 12px (buttons, panels), `lg` 18px (cards). Nothing else is cut.
- **Cards fold.** `@include r9.card-corner(lg)` turns the top-right corner over (26px at
  rest, `shape.card-cut`). Ninefold's `Card` and `TiltCard` draw the lining under the fold
  and lift it toward the pointer, up to 60px (`useCardLift` from `@rnine/core/kit`, set by
  `ThemeConfig.lift`).
- Edges are hairlines (`@include r9.hairline`). The only shadows are under a fold and
  under a letter.

## Ornaments

- The accent mark is a drop of ink (`@include r9.ornament`), round because the corners are.
- A drop of wax (`@include r9.wax-drop(10px)`) where it should look sealed: an eyebrow, a stop.
- The pen underline (`@include r9.pen-underline`) under links and quiet buttons.

## Paper

Every page sits on paper: pressed fibers (`texture(fibers)`) and a fine grain
(`texture(grain)`), fixed behind the content on `body::before` and `body::after`. In your
own pieces, put either on an empty layer:

```scss
.letter::before {
  content: "";
  position: absolute;
  inset: 0;
  @include r9.texture(fibers);   // in the dot role; or texture(fibers, $color: ...)
}
```

Lined paper (a letter, a form) is a repeating line in a tint of the text color, 34px apart.
